Eden Emulator - Setup
=====================

1. DOWNLOAD
   Eden-Emulator.zip from Releases (eden emulator github official).
   Android: install the eden emulator apk instead.

2. KEYS
   dump prod.keys and title.keys from your own Switch into keys/.
   the eden emulator keys and firmware reddit threads and the
   eden emulator prod keys reddit wiki explain the process.
   keys cannot be shared legally - dump your own.

3. FIRMWARE
   firmware 18.0.0+ files go into firmware/
   (firmware for eden emulator = standard switch firmware eden dumps).

4. GAMES
   point Eden at your dump folder. check the eden emulator
   compatibility list wiki before reporting a title.

5. PLAY
   launch a game. vulkan backend recommended.
   steam deck: eden_default controller profile + FSR on.

UPDATES
eden emulator nightly builds land daily - swap the executable,
saves/keys/settings carry over.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- AMD crash: fixed in v0.22
- TOTK audio desync: fixed in v0.22
- slow on android: snapdragon 8 gen 1+ recommended
- more help: eden emulator reddit community
